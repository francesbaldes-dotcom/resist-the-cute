## Description (2221 von 4000)

```text
You are the villain. Your enemies are adorable.

Kittens, bunnies, chicks and puppies are charging your lair to cuddle it. When the health bar of your tower runs out, the lair falls. Stop the fluff gang before that happens.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap a floor of your tower and build a gadget.
• Start the wave and watch your gadgets stop the animals.
• Every hit earns gold. Spend it on upgrades, more floors and heroes.
• Tap an animal and every gadget aims at it.

THREE GADGETS, MANY UPGRADES
The cannon hits one target, the slime launcher slows the attackers, the laser shoots through several animals at once. At gadget levels 10, 20 and 30 each one reaches a new tier with a new look and a new ability, such as double shot, sticky slime or chain laser.

YOUR VILLAIN EMPIRE
Add floors and balconies and reinforce your lair. In the village, the gold mine earns gold while a wave runs, the lab recharges energy for special powers, and the barracks trains orcs who hold animals back in front of your tower and tickle them.

SPECIAL POWERS AND HEROES
Sleep gas puts every animal to sleep, the cuddle bomb hits a whole crowd, the scare shout pushes them all back. With enough gold you hire heroes who stay with you forever: the champion at the front and the roof sniper with long range.

THE MASTER PLAN
A wheel of six schools with doctrines, keystones and masteries. Every boss drops a blueprint the first time you beat it, which strengthens nodes and is yours for good. Later you move to a new lair: your record stays, and dark seals buy you a head start in every new run.

OVER 30 KINDS OF ATTACKERS
Hopping hares, chick swarms, roosters that rally their friends, healers, dog packs that get angry when one falls, and balloon cats that fly out of reach of most gadgets. Plus 12 bosses with their own abilities, including the Cat King, the Hen Empress and the Grandmaster of Cute, and cat fortresses that send reinforcements. The further you get, the more the scenery changes.

AT A GLANCE
• Fully offline, no account needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• English and German
• For iPhone and iPad, in landscape
• Take your save to another device with a code
• Speed 1× or 2×; sound, music and haptics can each be switched off
```
